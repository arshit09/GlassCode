Talk to Claude Code through your glasses

You speak, your PC writes it down, Claude Code does the work on your PC, and the answer comes back as a voice in your glasses. At your desk, or from anywhere. No screen needed.

At your desk

The glasses connect to your PC by Bluetooth.

Swipe sideways to see the whole picture.

The black GlassesClaude window on your PC does all three steps. Your voice becomes text on your PC, and Claude Code works in your project folder just as if you had typed the request yourself.

When you're out

The glasses connect to your phone as usual. Your PC stays at home and does the work.

Swipe sideways to see the whole picture.

Say "in my portfolio project, fix the header spacing" or "create a new folder called pizza-site with a simple website". You hear "Got it" right away. When Claude is done, a notification arrives and the app plays the answer in your glasses. Your PC never opens a door to the internet: it simply asks the relay every few seconds.

Set it up once

  1. Double-click "3 - Set up away mode (optional)" It installs Node.js if needed, opens your browser once so you can sign in to Cloudflare (a free account, no credit card), puts your private relay online, and opens a page with a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone The GlassesClaude app opens with your private key. Use the browser menu to add it to your home screen.
  3. Turn on notifications Install the free ntfy app and subscribe to the topic shown on the QR page. In the GlassesClaude app, tap Settings, then Send a test.
  4. Reopen "2 - Start" and leave the PC on The black window now says it's connected to your relay. GlassesClaude keeps the PC awake while it runs. To start it automatically, run "Start GlassesClaude with Windows" in the tools folder.

Talk without touching the phone

Android

In the app's Settings, turn on "Start listening as soon as the app opens". Then say "Hey Google, open GlassesClaude" and start talking.

iPhone

Make a Shortcut called "Ask my PC": Dictate Text, then Get Contents of URL (POST to your relay's /api/ask?format=text with header X-Key and JSON field text set to the dictated text), then Speak Text. Say "Hey Siri, ask my PC". A second shortcut that gets /api/latest.txt and speaks it answers "what did Claude say?"

What it costs

  • Cloudflare Workers, free plan: 100,000 requests a day. Your PC uses about 17,000 by checking every 5 seconds.
  • ntfy.sh: free without an account. Your topic name works like a password, so keep it private.
  • Speech to text and the voice: free (Whisper on your PC, Microsoft's free online voice).
  • Claude Code: your existing plan, running on your PC.

Record now, run later

Record a voice memo with the glasses wherever you are. When the file lands in the inbox folder (share it from the Hi Rokid app to OneDrive or Google Drive, or use the ADB copy tool), GlassesClaude picks it up. A memo that starts with "Claude, ..." is run as a command. Any other memo is saved as a note. Results appear in inbox\done.

Get it running at your desk

  1. Double-click "1 - Install" It installs everything GlassesClaude needs into this folder, downloads the speech model (about 0.5 GB, once), and checks that Claude Code is installed and signed in. It's safe to run again. If Windows warns about running a downloaded file, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Pair the glasses with this PC Open Windows Settings, then Bluetooth & devices, then Add device, and put the glasses in pairing mode (see the Rokid manual). They show up as a headset with a microphone.
  3. Double-click "2 - Start" A black window opens. Keep it open: it shows which microphone and speaker it uses, and a link to the talk page.
  4. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space, talk, then pause You hear a beep, then "Got it", then Claude's answer. Press the keys again while you talk to send early, or while an answer plays to interrupt it.

No glasses connected yet? Open the talk-page link from the black window in your browser, click the button and talk into any microphone. It works the same way.

Things you can say

Work for Claude

Anything you would type into Claude Code:

  • "Create a new folder called pizza-site with a simple website."
  • "In my portfolio project, fix the header spacing on mobile."
  • "Run the tests and tell me what failed."
  • "Commit my changes with a good message."

Claude remembers the conversation, so you can follow up with "now push it".

Handled instantly

Say just these words on their own:

"stop"
cancels the task that is running
"new chat"
starts a fresh conversation
"repeat"
plays the last answer again
"status"
tells you what Claude is doing
"which project"
tells you which folder Claude works in
"switch to portfolio"
moves to a project you named in settings.toml

Choose the folder Claude works in

Claude starts in the practice folder workspace, inside GlassesClaude. To work on your real projects, open settings.toml with Notepad, put the folder in single quotes, save, and reopen "2 - Start". Pointing it at the folder that holds all your projects lets you say "in my portfolio project, ..." or "create a new folder ...":

project_folder = 'C:\Users\you\source\repos'

[projects]
portfolio = 'C:\Users\you\source\repos\my-portfolio'

Good to know

  • Claude runs in auto mode: it does the work, and a safety checker blocks risky actions. Nobody can click "Allow" while you're only talking, so anything that needs approval is skipped and Claude tells you. You can choose a stricter mode in settings.toml.
  • Spoken answers are kept short. The full answers are in logs\history.md and in the phone app.
  • Your voice is turned into text on your PC. The text goes to Claude Code. The answer text goes to Microsoft's free online voice to be read aloud; without internet, the built-in Windows voice is used instead.
  • When you're out, your requests and answers wait in your own Cloudflare relay and are deleted after 3 days. The notification includes the short answer; set answer_in_notification = false under [away] in settings.toml to keep answers out of notifications.
  • If your PC is off, a request waits up to 2 hours. After that it isn't run, and you get a notification saying so.
  • The talk-page link and the phone link contain private keys. Don't share them. Run the away-mode setup with --new-keys to replace the phone key.
  • Not every pair of glasses sends a tap to the PC. If tapping does nothing, use the keys.
  • Close the black window to stop everything.

If something doesn't work

The window says the microphone isn't your glasses
Make sure the glasses are paired and connected in Windows Bluetooth settings, then reopen "2 - Start". If it still picks another microphone, the black window lists the microphones it can see: put part of the glasses' name in settings.toml as microphone and speaker.
Claude Code: NOT FOUND
Open PowerShell and run irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex, then type claude once to sign in, and reopen "2 - Start".
The phone app says "PC offline"
Check that "2 - Start" is running and the PC is on and online. The black window should say it's connected to your relay.
No notification arrives
In the ntfy app, check the topic matches the one on the QR page exactly, then use Send a test in the GlassesClaude app's Settings.
Away-mode setup asks for a workers.dev address
Open the Cloudflare dashboard, go to Workers & Pages, pick a free workers.dev subdomain, then run the setup again.
The keys do nothing
Another program may use the same keys, or a program running as administrator is in front. Pick other keys in settings.toml, for example hotkey = "f8".
Claude says it couldn't do a step
In auto mode risky steps are blocked. Do that step yourself, or change permission_mode in settings.toml.

What's in this folder

1 - InstallSets everything up on this PC. Safe to run again.
2 - StartStarts GlassesClaude. Keep its window open.
3 - Set up away mode (optional)Puts your free relay on Cloudflare and makes the phone link.
settings.tomlYour settings: project folder, keys, voice, safety mode, away mode.
workspaceClaude's practice folder until you choose a project.
inboxDrop voice memos here. Results go to inbox\done.
logs\history.mdEverything you said and every answer (created on first use).
relayThe free cloud relay and the phone app (uploaded by the away-mode setup).
toolsStart with Windows, and optional ADB helpers for glasses that allow a USB connection.
appThe program itself.
Made for display-free AI glasses such as Rokid AI Glasses Style. It works with any Bluetooth headset that has a microphone.
